## PR #54 — Plain-reader copy: shorter subtitles, friendly dates, no em-dashes
*merged 2026-08-25 · +71/−48*

### PR body

## What changed

Copy and readability pass over the interactive county view, extended site-wide where the same issues recur. All changes are in this repo; nothing rendered by uisce needed a statusui edit (the bar caption is assembled locally in `describeCell`).

**County view (`site.html`)**
- The ~1,000-char "Areas" paragraph is now one line ("**Select an area**…") with the Census placement / "Around …" / no-letter-grades detail behind a "How areas are drawn" `<details>` disclosure. The word "pin" no longer appears in reader-facing copy anywhere.
- "Pick your county for details." in the header is now a span hidden on every view except the overview.
- "Open right now" lost its "this list does not change with the month you are viewing" clause.
- The "Notices that closed in <month>" subtitle is removed entirely; its one useful caveat now lives as a short "When a notice closed" paragraph in the footer's "How these numbers are worked out" disclosure.

**Dates**
- Bar captions read "Sat 1 Aug: Supply disruption · about 0.1% of the county" instead of ISO 8601, via a new `fmtDay` helper. Both bar render sites (overview rows and the county drill-down) go through `describeCell`, so one change covers them.
- The same treatment applies to the open/closed incident lists, area histories, the top ten, and — via a matching `_fmt_day` in `site.py` — the static `c/*.html` pages. The year is appended only when it isn't the current year.

**Em-dashes**
- Replaced with hyphens (or recast with commas where that read better) in all user-visible prose across `site.html`, `areas.html`, `county.html`, and `site.py`'s generated strings. Kept: the bare "—" empty-cell placeholders (a table convention, deliberately retained) and code comments.

**Duplicate prose**
- The area-history note, `areas.html` footer, and `county.html` footer no longer restate the pin-placement rule (previously worded four ways); the canonical version is the county-view disclosure plus the methodology notes.

## Why

The county page read as written for the site's author, not its average reader: methodology-flavoured subtitles, jargon ("pin"), ISO dates, and em-dashes throughout. Hard facts are kept but demoted to disclosures.

The footer loses its HRs, the disclosures are rewritten for lay readers, and it ends in the shared "Source code · not affiliated with Uisce Éireann." line.

Tests that pinned behaviour through coloured quality days were repurposed to assert the same facts through `health_now`, month membership and `boil_notice_fate`; a new guard pins that quality days do not colour the bars. Branch review (high effort) ran clean apart from one copy fix: the static county pages said "elapsed days with no notice", which a month with a standing boil notice would contradict in the same sentence — they now say "clear of supply disruption". 443 tests green.
