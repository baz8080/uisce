## PR #44 — Show the hovered day's caption under each county's bar, and drop the tooltips
*merged 2026-08-19 · +18/−9*

### PR body

## What

- The county list now shows the hovered day's status as a line under that county's bar, immediately — the readout the county page's big bar already had.
- The per-day `title=` tooltips are removed (they said the same thing after a delay, and never on a phone), and so is the row-level `title`, which would otherwise start appearing over the bar once the cell titles stopped masking it.
- On touch devices the list caption is hidden (`@media (hover: none)`): a tap on a cell bubbles to the row and navigates, and cells are ~8px wide — far under any usable touch target — so there is nothing for it to show and no reason to reserve the strip. The county page's own bar keeps its tap readout, since nothing navigates there.

## Why the caption sits inside `.row`

esb/lifts put `<div class="daycap">` beside `.row` inside `.county`, with the hover grey on `.row:hover`, so the grey band stops above the caption. Here it's a full-width third grid row inside `.row` (`grid-column: 1 / -1`), so the row's hover band, click target and `:focus-visible` ring cover it by construction. Same fix would apply to esb and lifts if wanted.

## Verified

- Desktop: hovering a cell fills that county's caption instantly; grey band covers name, bar, stats and caption as one block; no tooltip anywhere on the list.
- Touch emulation (375px): caption hidden, no reserved height, row still navigates on tap.
- Narrow layout with a mouse: caption sits under the bar spanning the card.
- County page bar readout unchanged; no `title` on cells.
- `uv run pytest`: 374 passed.

- `notes/frontend-notes.md` and a CLAUDE.md row record the split and the change loop. Sister PRs: [esb#3](https://github.com/baz8080/esb/pull/3), [lifts#4](https://github.com/baz8080/lifts/pull/4).
