Every area with a notice

Every Census 2022 area Uisce Éireann has published a supply or works notice for since 20 April 2026 — pick one for its full incident history. The two figures beside each name are notices published and people living there.

An area is a CSO settlement, a Local Electoral Area of one of the five cities, or the countryside of an Electoral Division shown as Around …. A notice is placed in the area its affected population centres on, so an event whose map pins span two areas is listed under each of them and its count appears in both — the figure beside each name is how many notices that area has seen, not a share of a national total.

Areas with no notice at all are not listed: roughly 1,900 of Ireland's named areas have had none since collection began, and a page of them would say nothing. Source and methodology.
