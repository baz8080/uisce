Every area with a notice

Every Census 2022 area Uisce Éireann has published a supply or works notice for. Pick one for its full incident history.

An area is a CSO settlement, a Local Electoral Area of one of the five cities, or the countryside of an Electoral Division shown as Around …. An event that affects two areas is listed under each of them, so the figure beside each name is how many notices that area has seen, not a share of a national total.

Areas with no notice at all are not listed: roughly 1,900 of Ireland's named areas have had none since collection began, and a page of them would say nothing. Source and methodology.
