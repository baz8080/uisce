← Irish Water Supply Disruptions

Supply availability is the population-weighted share of people's time not covered by an announced supply disruption; the letter grade is calibrated to it and to nothing else. An active boil-water or do-not-drink notice is published beside the grade rather than folded into it. Person-hours count population-weighted hours of lost supply.

A notice is placed in the area its affected population centres on, so an event whose map pins span two areas is listed under each of them. The count beside an area name is how many notices that area has seen. Source and methodology · every area in Ireland.
